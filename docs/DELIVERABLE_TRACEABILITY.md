## Scope notes from supplied reference

The attached PDF is treated as design material. Its season-specific `DRIVER_TEAM` relationship and separation of high-frequency telemetry into MongoDB are useful inputs. The full project brief additionally requires security, audit, strategy and analytical entities, so the final schema may extend the PDF. The vector database is conditional in the brief; its inclusion will be justified rather than assumed.
